What Quested Is

Quested brings the familiar rhythm of a classic quest journal to everyday tasks. It keeps the fantasy in the framing, without XP, combat, fake currency, character stats, or other game systems.

  • Organize Life Into Quests

    Daily, Main, and Side Quests give different kinds of tasks a natural place.

  • Build Daily Rituals

    A completed repeating Daily Quest returns as a new quest for the next local calendar day.

  • Keep It Simple

    Create, edit, complete, restore, set due dates, and move quests between categories with familiar iOS interactions.

Support

For help with Quested, email evenmatterdev@gmail.com.

How does Repeat Daily work?

When you complete a repeating Daily Quest, Quested prepares a new one for the next local calendar day. It appears when the app next opens or returns to the foreground.

How do I restore a completed quest?

In the Completed section, swipe right on the quest to restore it to the active list.

Where is my quest data stored?

Quest information is stored locally on your device. If you enable Apple device backups, app data may be included according to your backup settings.

How do I contact support?

Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

About Quested

Quested is an independently developed iOS application published under the name Evenmatter.

Information stored in the app

Quested stores the quest information you enter on your device using Apple’s SwiftData technology. This can include quest titles and details, categories, creation and update dates, optional due dates, completion dates, and Repeat Daily settings. A local app preference is also used to manage the starter quests shown when you first open the app. Quest information normally remains in the app’s local storage until you delete the relevant quest or remove Quested and its associated local app data.

Accounts, transmission, and sale

Quested does not provide an account system or a developer-controlled cloud synchronization service, and does not transmit quest content to a developer-operated server. The app does not currently use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party telemetry SDKs. The developer does not sell users’ personal information.

Apple services and backups

Quested uses Apple platform frameworks to store and present your quests. Depending on your settings, Apple-managed device backups may include app data and retain it under Apple’s backup practices. Deleting a quest in Quested does not necessarily remove copies from those backups; the developer does not control backup copies managed by Apple.

Deleting quest information

You can delete an individual quest by swiping left on it. Removing Quested and its associated local app data removes the app’s local quest store. Quested has no online account or developer-operated server-side quest record to delete. Any copies included in Apple-managed backups remain subject to your backup settings and Apple’s retention practices.

Contacting support

Contacting support is voluntary. If you email evenmatterdev@gmail.com, you send the developer the information you choose to include, which may be used to respond to your inquiry. Your email service provider also handles the message under its own privacy practices. The website’s mail link opens your email application; the website does not automatically transmit quest data. The developer has not specified a fixed retention period for support messages.

Children and privacy

Quested has no account system, online community, or feature for submitting information to an online service. Parents or guardians with a privacy question can contact the developer using the email below.

Website hosting

The Quested website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about website visits under its own privacy practices. This may include IP addresses, device or browser details, timestamps, pages visited, and referral information. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for information about its handling of that data. This hosting is separate from the Quested iOS app, which does not send users’ quest content to GitHub.

Changes and contact

If Quested’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed app version ships. The effective date above will be revised when the policy changes. Questions about Quested or this policy can be sent to evenmatterdev@gmail.com.
